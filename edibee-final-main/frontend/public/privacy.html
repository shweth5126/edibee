Privacy Policy.

Last updated: 31 August 2026

Who we are

Edibee Media ("we", "us") is a creative growth agency. This site is edibee.vercel.app. Contact: edibee.grow@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Contact form: the name, email address and project details you choose to submit. This is delivered to our email inbox through Web3Forms, a form-to-email service. We use it only to reply to your enquiry.
  • WhatsApp: if you message us via the WhatsApp links, your message and number are handled by WhatsApp under its own policy.
  • Analytics: we use Vercel Analytics, which counts page views and basic device/region data without cookies and without identifying you.

What we don't do

We don't sell or rent your data, we don't run advertising trackers, and we don't store form submissions in a database on this site.

Your choices

To ask what we hold about you, or to have your enquiry data deleted, email edibee.grow@gmail.com and we'll action it within 30 days.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above reflects the latest version.
